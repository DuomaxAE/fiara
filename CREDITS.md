# Visual credits & usage notes

## Supplied product photography
All files in `assets/products/` are resized/compressed versions of the product photographs supplied in the conversation by the user. These are used as the primary product visuals for the demo site.

## External visual references
The site references Pexels-hosted photography for visual direction and fallback editorial imagery only. The referenced source pages state the images are “Free to use”:

- https://www.pexels.com/photo/luxurious-gift-package-of-various-sweets-20699855/
- https://www.pexels.com/photo/chocolates-in-a-box-18434553/
- https://www.pexels.com/photo/festive-gift-box-with-assorted-sweets-and-cookies-28769885/

For production, download/cache the exact assets you decide to use and re-check the applicable Pexels license/terms before publishing.
